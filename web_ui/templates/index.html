Click two points across the road to draw the red stop line. Vehicles crossing this line during a RED signal are recorded as infractions.

Select Traffic Signal Point Click
Click directly on the traffic light in the video or zoom in for precision. Point selection automatically centers the ROI target box over the lamp housing.
Click directly on traffic signal lamp

Click boundary points to focus surveillance on specific lanes. Double-click to close polygon (defaults to full video frame if omitted).
Click lane points, double-click to finish
4
Set Flow Direction Optional
Drag an arrow along the legal road direction. If left unconfigured, wrong-way detection remains disabled to prevent false alarms.
Drag arrow in allowable flow direction

  • Press Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y or use Undo/Redo buttons to adjust points anytime.
