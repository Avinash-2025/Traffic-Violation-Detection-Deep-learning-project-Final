Multi-Model Detection Architecture

Vehicle Detection
Fixed YOLOv5 backbone tracks cars, buses, trucks, motorcycles, and auto-rickshaws with SORT multi-target Kalman tracking.
Helmet & Triple Riding
Dual-head classifier identifies helmet compliance and counts passengers on motorcycles to flag overcrowded two-wheelers.
Stop Line & Red Light
Dynamic road stop line geometry combined with HSV signal color classification flags illegal intersection crossings.
Directional Flow Geometry
Vehicle trajectory vectors compared against allowable roadway direction vectors automatically flag wrong-way drivers.

MARKUP SETUP GUIDE
1 Draw Stop Line
Click two points on the road to place the red stop line. Vehicles crossing this line during a RED signal are recorded as infractions.
Click Stop Line tool, then click 2 points on the road →
2 Select Traffic Signal (Point Click)
Click directly on the traffic light in the video or zoom in for precision. Point selection automatically centers the ROI target box over the lamp housing.
Click directly on the traffic signal lamp →
3 Detection Area (Optional)
Click boundary points to focus vehicle surveillance on specific road lanes. Double-click to close polygon (defaults to full video frame).
Click points around road zone, double-click to finish →
4 Set Traffic Direction (Optional)
Drag an arrow in the permitted road flow direction. If left unconfigured, wrong-way detection remains disabled to prevent false positives.
Drag arrow from start to end in flow direction →
